What holds the team together

  • Stronger togetherA bundle of reeds cannot be broken. A team can do more than its people alone.
  • We answer for each otherThe whole team is accountable for the result, not one person.
  • Equals at one tableWe decide together, and every voice carries the same weight.
  • Contribution is visibleRecognition for what you gave, not for your title.
  • Benefit to people is the measureWe take on only what is truly needed.

Which tasks we take on

We score every task on seven questions, from 0 to 2, so teams spend their effort on what is truly needed.

A task passes with 10 of 14 points and no zero on necessity, harm or payment.

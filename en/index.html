Frequently asked questions

What kind of tasks do you solve?

Problems of small and mid-sized companies that cost time and money: processes, automation, customer handling, data, launching new lines of business. We take on tasks that pass our filter: people need them and are willing to pay.

Where do you work?

In Dubai and remotely worldwide. We work in English, Russian and other languages — choose yours in the form.

How does it start and what does it cost?

With a free 30-minute conversation. Then a two-week diagnostic at a fixed price: you receive an analysis of the problem and a solution plan with price and timeline. After that, work proceeds in stages, each priced up front.

Who is accountable for the result?

The whole team. The Team Lead is your single contact, but every member stands behind the result.

What if a stage is not right?

You accept every stage. If a stage is not accepted, the team finishes it at its own cost.

Can I take part if I have a main job?

Yes. We match your role to the time you can give, from a couple of hours a week to full-time.
